Unmask
A risk check, not proof. Unmask can be wrong: it can miss a fake and it can raise a false alarm. It never ends calls, contacts anyone, or identifies the caller. Clips are deleted within 24 hours.

Sharing a call window only works in desktop Chrome or Edge, not on phones or tablets. You can still try the samples or upload a recording.

Check a live caller

  1. Open your call (WhatsApp Web, Meet, Zoom…) in another window or tab.
  2. Press Share the call window and pick that window. Make the caller's video big.
  3. Unmask records short clips and tells you what to ask the caller to do.

Optional: drag on the preview to select just the caller's video tile.
